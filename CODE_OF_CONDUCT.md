# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, harassment-free community. Be constructive, assume
good intent while examining evidence, respect differing experience levels, and do not
publish private information or security reports.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sustained personal
attacks, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, and retaliation against good-faith
reporting. Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, restrict participation, or ban
participants when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through the repository's private
contact channel once the public repository is created. Until then, do not include
sensitive personal details in a public issue. Maintainers will acknowledge reports,
avoid conflicts of interest, and communicate outcomes as privacy permits.
